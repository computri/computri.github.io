About me

I'm a PhD candidate at the Amsterdam Machine Learning Lab (University of Amsterdam), supervised by Erik Bekkers as part of the GRaMLab.

My research focuses on applications of geometric deep learning for vision tasks—particularly on making models robust to geometric transformations in a way that is both efficient and reliable. In relation to the latter, I’ve recently developed an interest in uncertainty quantification in deep learning. I apply these methods in the context of computer vision and 3D data, such as point clouds.

Before starting my PhD, I completed both my BSc and MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam, where I focused on statistics, deep learning, and computer vision. During that time, I had the pleasure of doing my master's thesis under the supervision of Karen Ullrich, where we explored neural network sparsification in the generative setting of variational autoencoders using Bayesian neural networks.

Feel free to reach out via LinkedIn or email—always happy to connect over a coffee and share my CV upon request :)
